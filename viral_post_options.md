## RECOMMENDATION

**Use Version 1** as your primary post.

**Why:**
1. Shortest (best for mobile dwell time)
2. Most quotable ("signal louder than noise")
3. Direct CTA ("RT if you remember")
4. Emotional peak at end (cat emoji + nostalgia)
5. Perfect balance of data + emotion

**Timing Strategy:**
- Post during US peak hours (9-11am PT or 5-7pm PT)
- Tuesday-Thursday = highest engagement days
- Avoid Monday (low), Weekend (mixed)

**Engagement Strategy:**
- Don't reply immediately (let organic replies build)
- After 1-2 hours, engage with top replies
- Quote-retweet supportive responses
- Pin to profile

**Boost Strategy:**
- Ask 3-5 mid-tier accounts (10K-100K followers) to repost in first hour
- Not verified/huge accounts (looks coordinated)
- Mid-tier = authentic amplification

## Algorithm Insights Used

Based on X's open-source recommendation algorithm:

1. **favorite_score** (likes) = highest weight → emotional hook
2. **reply_score** = second highest → question at end
3. **retweet_score** = direct CTA ("RT if...")
4. **quote_score** = quotable phrase ("signal louder than noise")
5. **dwell_time** = continuous metric → short but dense paragraphs
6. **author_diversity** = first post from you gets full weight
7. **recency** = Thunder prioritizes new posts (timing matters)
8. **negative signals** avoided = no spam patterns, no blocks/reports
